ALMA

Built on Project Aria Gen 2 · {{ hackathon }}

Your lab assistant, built into your glasses.

ALMA sees the bench through Aria Gen 2 glasses. It knows lab equipment and procedures, guides researchers through each protocol, flags mistakes as they happen and writes the report for them.

Pipette tracking on 96-well plates

How it works

Wear the glasses. Run the protocol. Get the report.

Bench work shouldn't stop for paperwork. ALMA documents each experiment from the researcher's point of view, so their hands stay on the work.

  1. 1

    Wear

    Put on the Aria Gen 2 glasses and load your protocol. ALMA needs only the glasses' camera.

  2. 2

    Work

    Run the experiment as usual. ALMA recognises the equipment and actions at the bench and checks each step against the protocol.

  3. 3

    Review

    Finish with a drafted lab report: every step timestamped and any deviations noted, ready to check and edit.

Core feature

Automated lab reports

ALMA turns first-person video into a structured record of the experiment: what was done, in what order and with which equipment. The report is written during the work, not afterwards from memory.

  • Timestamped, step-by-step log
  • Equipment used at each step
  • Draft report ready when the work is done
Core feature

Protocol mistake detection

Load a protocol and ALMA follows along. When the work departs from it, such as a skipped step or steps done out of order, ALMA points it out straight away, before it costs a run.

  • Follows the protocol step by step
  • Flags deviations as they happen
  • Every deviation recorded in the report
